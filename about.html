About Mkhulu's

This is a fast food place that sells delicious kotas at Etwatwa that will leave you sucking your fingers after eating. It also sells ribs, fries, wings and other finger foods. They have a delivery service via Mashesha Deliveries with a proximity that extends to Crystal Park and Putfontein.
